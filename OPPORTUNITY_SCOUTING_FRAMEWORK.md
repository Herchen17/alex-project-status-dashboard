# OPPORTUNITY SCOUTING EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

## 🎯 SYSTEMATIC OPPORTUNITY ASSESSMENT

### **Evaluation Matrix (1-10 Scale)**

#### **BUILD COMPLEXITY SCORE (Lower = Better)**
- **1-3**: Simple web app, basic CRUD, existing templates
- **4-6**: Custom features, API integrations, moderate complexity  
- **7-8**: Complex algorithms, AI integration, multiple systems
- **9-10**: Novel technology, research required, high technical risk

#### **PROFIT POTENTIAL SCORE (Higher = Better)**
- **1-3**: Unclear monetization, small market, high competition
- **4-6**: Moderate market size, established revenue model
- **7-8**: Large market, multiple revenue streams, growth potential
- **9-10**: Exceptional market opportunity, scalable, first-mover advantage

#### **AUTONOMOUS BUILD SCORE (Higher = Better)**
- **1-3**: Requires significant human creativity/judgment
- **4-6**: Some manual intervention needed, moderate automation
- **7-8**: Mostly automatable, clear requirements, minimal oversight
- **9-10**: Fully automatable, template-driven, systematic approach

## 🔍 RED FLAGS (AUTOMATIC DISQUALIFICATION)

### **Technical Red Flags**:
- ❌ Requires physical hardware/inventory
- ❌ Heavy regulation compliance (finance, medical, legal)
- ❌ Real-time critical systems (life safety, trading)
- ❌ Novel AI research (beyond current capabilities)

### **Business Red Flags**:  
- ❌ Requires significant capital investment (>$1K)
- ❌ Needs specialized domain expertise we don't have
- ❌ Market completely saturated with established players
- ❌ Revenue model unproven or unrealistic

### **Execution Red Flags**:
- ❌ Requires full-time human customer service
- ❌ Complex fulfillment/logistics
- ❌ Legal/IP risks too high
- ❌ Timeline to profitability >6 months

## ✅ GREEN FLAGS (PRIORITY INDICATORS)

### **Technical Green Flags**:
- ✅ Web-based solution (our strength)
- ✅ API-driven architecture  
- ✅ Existing tools/frameworks available
- ✅ Clear technical requirements
- ✅ Automation-friendly workflows

### **Business Green Flags**:
- ✅ Clear monetization path
- ✅ Low competition in niche
- ✅ Proven market demand
- ✅ Subscription/recurring revenue potential
- ✅ B2B market (higher margins)

### **Execution Green Flags**:
- ✅ MVP buildable in <30 days
- ✅ Low operational overhead
- ✅ Self-service user experience
- ✅ Scalable without proportional cost increase
- ✅ Complements existing skills/projects

## 📊 SCORING ALGORITHM

```
Final Score = (
  (11 - Build Complexity) × 0.3 +  // Invert complexity so lower = better
  Profit Potential × 0.4 +
  Autonomous Score × 0.3
) × 10

Score Interpretation:
90-100: Exceptional opportunity - immediate investigation
80-89:  High potential - detailed analysis warranted  
70-79:  Moderate opportunity - monitor trends
60-69:  Low priority - casual tracking
<60:    Not worth pursuing
```
