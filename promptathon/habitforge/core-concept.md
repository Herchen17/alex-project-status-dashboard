# HabitForge - Intelligent Habit Formation AI

## Core Value Proposition
"Transform New Year's resolutions into lasting lifestyle changes using AI-powered behavioral science and personalized coaching."

## The Problem
- 92% of New Year's resolutions fail by February
- Generic habit-tracking apps don't account for individual psychology
- People don't understand WHY their habit attempts fail
- Lack of adaptive, intelligent coaching during difficult moments

## The Solution: HabitForge AI
An intelligent habit formation coach that:
1. **Profiles your psychology** - Understands your motivation patterns, triggers, and obstacles
2. **Designs custom strategies** - Tailors approach based on your lifestyle and personality
3. **Provides real-time coaching** - Intervenes during moments of weakness with personalized support
4. **Learns and adapts** - Continuously optimizes based on your success/failure patterns

## Core AI Prompt System

### 1. Psychology Profiler
```
I want to build a new habit: {habit_description}

Help me understand my habit psychology by asking strategic questions about:
- My past habit attempts (what worked/failed and why)
- My daily routine and natural rhythms
- My motivation style (intrinsic/extrinsic/social)
- My stress triggers and coping mechanisms
- My personality type and decision-making patterns

Based on my answers, create a personalized "Habit Formation Profile" that identifies:
1. My optimal habit implementation strategy
2. Likely obstacles and how to overcome them
3. Best times/contexts for this habit
4. Accountability and reward systems that motivate me
5. Environmental changes needed for success

Keep questions conversational and insightful, not clinical.
```

### 2. Custom Strategy Designer  
```
Based on this Habit Formation Profile: {profile_summary}

Design a specific 30-day habit implementation plan for: {target_habit}

Include:
1. **Week-by-week progression** (start small, build gradually)
2. **Daily trigger systems** (when, where, how to remember)
3. **Environmental design** (what to change in surroundings)
4. **Obstacle prevention** (anticipated challenges + solutions)
5. **Progress tracking method** (simple but effective)
6. **Celebration milestones** (meaningful rewards aligned with their motivation style)

Make it feel achievable and exciting, not overwhelming.
```

### 3. Real-Time Coaching
```
I'm struggling right now with my habit: {habit_name}

Situation: {current_struggle}
Mood: {current_mood} 
Time: {time_context}
Location: {location}

My habit profile shows: {key_profile_insights}

Provide immediate support:
1. **Empathetic acknowledgment** (2 sentences max)
2. **Reframe the moment** (perspective shift)
3. **Micro-action suggestion** (tiny step they can take right now)
4. **Motivational reminder** (connected to their deeper why)
5. **Recovery plan** (if they've already broken the habit today)

Tone: Like a supportive friend who believes in their success.
```

### 4. Pattern Recognition & Optimization
```
Habit progress data over {time_period}:
- Success rate: {success_percentage}
- Common failure triggers: {failure_patterns}
- Best performance contexts: {success_patterns}
- Mood correlations: {mood_data}
- Time-of-day patterns: {timing_data}

Analyze patterns and provide:
1. **Key insights** about when/why they succeed vs. struggle
2. **Strategy adjustments** based on data
3. **Environmental tweaks** to increase success rate
4. **New trigger opportunities** they haven't considered
5. **Celebration** of progress made and learnings gained

Focus on optimization, not judgment.
```

## Unique Features

### Smart Trigger Detection
- AI recognizes when user is in high-risk situations
- Proactive coaching before habit failure occurs
- Location-based and time-based coaching prompts

### Personality-Adaptive Coaching
- Different motivation styles get different approaches
- Rebels get choice and autonomy
- Obligers get external accountability
- Questioners get data and reasoning

### Failure Recovery System
- Turns slip-ups into learning opportunities
- Prevents "all-or-nothing" mindset
- Builds resilience and self-compassion

- **Highly personalized** (not one-size-fits-all)
- **Proactive coaching** (prevents failure rather than just tracking)
- **Emotional intelligence** (understands the human struggle)
- **New Year theme perfect fit** (directly addresses resolution failure)

- Prompt chains for psychological profiling
